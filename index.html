How this works

    The same three colours, everywhere

    • Sage — that is it. What you named is what was played.
    • Gold — close. The next band along, a decibel out, the right depth and the wrong timing. What "close" means is each tool's own business, and the second column spells it out.
    • Rust — not close. Never a mark against you: it is how far the two things sit apart.
    • Try it as many times as you like. Nothing runs out. Hint says what kind of move it is; Show me draws the answer on the tool and leaves the controls live.
    • You are marked on the result, not the settings. Two different sets of controls that make the same curve, the same decay or the same harmonic series are the same answer. What is compared is what came out.
    • Everything is kept in this browser. No account, no server.
