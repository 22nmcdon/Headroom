Headroom

what this device can run

Open this on the device you care about — a phone is the point of it. It renders Headroom's own DSP offline and times it: load is the share of one core a second of audio costs, so 0.5 means half a core and anything over 1.0 cannot be done in real time at all. The saturator is the one to watch; at eight times oversampling with a 257-tap filter it costs about twelve times what the compressor or the imager do.
